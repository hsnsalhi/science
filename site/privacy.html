Privacy Policy

Eurêka mobile app
Version of 15 October 2026

In one sentence

Eurêka collects no personal data, transmits none, and makes no network requests whatsoever. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

1. Publisher

The Eurêka app is published by:

To complete before release: legal name, status, postal address and company registration number where applicable.

Contact: contact@techlyb.com

2. Data we collect

None. Eurêka has:

  • no account creation and no sign-up;
  • no request for an email address, phone number, date of birth or postal address;
  • no advertising identifier and no device identifier collection;
  • no analytics, no audience measurement, no automatic crash reporting;
  • no advertising, and therefore no advertising trackers;
  • no system permission requests: no location, contacts, camera, microphone or notifications.

We operate no server that receives user data, and the app embeds no third-party data-collection SDK.

3. Data stored on your device

For the app to work, some information is written to the app's private storage area, on your device only.

InformationPurpose
School level and daily goalSurface the right content
First name, optional Show a greeting on the home screen. May be left empty and cleared at any time
Experience points, rank, day streak Track progress and the daily goal
Per-lesson results and practice days Compute mastery stars and suggest the next lesson
Display settings (theme, motion, haptics, text size) Remember your comfort preferences

This information never leaves the device. It is not transmitted, not backed up to any server, and not shared with anyone. It is not accessible to other apps.

4. No transfers, no third parties

Eurêka makes no network connection, including on first launch: all educational content ships inside the app. No data is therefore sent to the publisher, to any processor, ad network or analytics provider. No transfer outside the European Union takes place, because no transfer takes place at all.

5. Children

Eurêka is designed for students, including primary-school pupils. Because no personal data is collected, no parental consent is required under the GDPR, and the app meets, by construction, the requirements applicable to services directed to children, including COPPA. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no outbound links and no way to communicate with third parties.

6. Your rights

The GDPR grants you rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability over your personal data. We hold no data about you, so on our side there is nothing to disclose, correct, export or delete.

You retain full control over what is stored on your device:

  • Erase progress: Settings → My data → "Reset my progress".
  • Erase everything: uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored information.

Questions: contact@techlyb.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

7. This website

These pages are static. They set no cookies, use no analytics, and load no resource from a third-party service — the webfont is served from this same domain precisely so your IP address is not exposed elsewhere. As with any site, the host may keep technical access logs for security and operations.

8. Changes

This policy may change if the app changes. Any revised version is published on this page with its date. Should a future version of the app ever collect any data — which is not planned — this would be announced here and in the app before it takes effect.
